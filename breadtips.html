🍞 Bread Tips & Recipe Guide

Your go-to reference for mastering soft, fluffy bread — the kind that makes you proud to slice into it.

Top 10 Tips for Softer Bread

1. Increase Hydration

Tip: Try increasing the water slightly. A hydration level of around 65%–70% (water weight relative to flour) is ideal. For 570 g of flour, 380 ml gives ~66%. If dough can handle it, add 10–20 ml more water.

2. Use the Tangzhong (Water Roux) Method

What it does: Locks in moisture for a super-soft crumb.

How to do it: Mix 10% of flour (≈ 57 g) with 5× its weight in water (≈ 285 ml). Heat while stirring until thick, then cool before adding to dough.

3. Add More Fat

Tip: 1–2 tbsp butter or olive oil enriches the crumb and flavour.

4. Include More Dairy

Tip: 3–4 tbsp full-cream milk powder or replace part of water with whole milk for creamier dough.

5. Incorporate Eggs

Tip: One egg (~50 g) adds protein and richness. Reduce water by 25–30 ml to balance.

6. Adjust Rising Time

Tip: Don't overproof — aim for 80% of maximum rise on the second proof for a good oven spring.

7. Add a Sweetener

Tip: 1–2 tbsp honey, sugar, or malt syrup retains moisture and feeds yeast.

8. Steam During Baking

What it does: Keeps surface moist for better oven spring.

How: Tray of hot water on bottom rack or mist oven walls before baking.

9. Adjust Baking Temperature

Tip: Bake at 180°C (355°F) for 30–35 min rather than a long low bake — retains moisture.

10. Let It Cool Properly

Tip: Cool on a rack, cover with a clean tea towel to trap gentle steam and soften crust.

🍞 Tangzhong (Water Roux) Method

What it does: Cooked flour + water/milk mixture retains moisture, giving a soft, fluffy crumb and longer shelf life.

Ingredients:

  • 57 g Bakers Flour (~10% of total flour)
  • 285 ml Water or Milk

Instructions:

  1. Whisk flour and water until smooth. Heat over medium stirring constantly.
  2. Mixture thickens to pudding-like consistency (~65°C / 150°F).
  3. Cool to room temperature before adding to dough.
  4. Use as part of the liquid in bread — e.g., 285 ml in Tangzhong + 95 ml in main dough for Grain & Seed Bread.

★ Tip

Can be made ahead and stored in fridge up to 2 days. Bring to room temp before use.

📌 Method 1: Classic (Without Tangzhong)

  1. Mix dry ingredients (flour, salt, bread improver, yeast, milk powder).
  2. Add wet ingredients (water, olive oil, egg), mix to dough.
  3. Knead 8–10 min until smooth/elastic. Gluten-Free: knead until combined.
  4. First rise: cover, let double (~1–1.5 hrs).
  5. Punch down, shape, place in greased tins.
  6. Second rise: 30–45 min.
  7. Bake 180°C (350°F) 25–35 min (rolls 18–25 min). Gluten-Free slightly longer.
  8. Cool 20 min on wire rack; brush with butter.

📌 Method 2: Tangzhong (With Tangzhong)

  1. Prepare Tangzhong: 30 g flour + 150 ml water/milk, cook low heat to paste, cool.
  2. Mix remaining dry ingredients (flour minus Tangzhong flour, salt, bread improver, yeast, milk powder).
  3. Add wet ingredients (adjusted water, olive oil, egg) + cooled Tangzhong, mix to dough.
  4. Knead 8–10 min until smooth/elastic. Gluten-Free: knead until combined.
  5. First rise: cover, let double (~1–1.5 hrs).
  6. Punch down, shape, second rise 30–45 min.
  7. Bake 180°C (350°F) 25–35 min (rolls 18–25 min). Gluten-Free slightly longer.
  8. Cool 20 min on wire rack; brush with butter.

★ Notes

Using Tangzhong gives softer, fluffier crumb and longer freshness. Adjust water slightly depending on flour type and humidity.
