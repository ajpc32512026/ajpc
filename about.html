Our Story

Ana & John's
Kitchen Notebook

Not a blog, not a brand — just the recipes we actually cook, written down so they don't get lost.

Once upon a kitchen, there was John — a cook by habit, instinct, and stubborn repetition. With over 30 years in the kitchen since 1992, including 15 years as a commercial short order and à la carte cook, every dish he makes has a purpose: food that works, feeds properly, and never needs explaining twice. By his side is Ana, the eager taster, cheerleader, and occasional critic. She mostly observes from the other room, offering encouragement, tasting bites at perfect moments, and passing judgment with a grin. Together, they form a quiet, unstoppable culinary duo.

From the hum of everyday meals to the rituals of baking and roasting, they've built AJPC (Ana & John's Personal Collection)— a collection of recipes honed through repetition, trial, and sometimes gentle swearing until every dish behaves. There are no fleeting trends here, no flashy fads. Instead, you'll find the timeless rhythms of bread, pastry, sauces, roasts, and sweets — the techniques and flavours that endure because they simply work.

Most recipes have origins worth noting. When they borrow a recipe, credit is given plainly and honestly. When there's no acknowledgment, it means the recipe has been adapted — adjusted for taste, sometimes less salt, more sugar, or just a little extra butter. AJPC captures this process, preserving each tweak, each improvement, so the good stuff is never lost.

Welcome to AJPC. Welcome to the kitchen where tradition meets experimentation, where every recipe tells a story, and where good food is always ready, repeatable, and made with care.
