🌟 How to Bloom Gelatin

A simple but essential technique for smooth, lump-free cold-set cheesecakes. Treat gelatin right, and it will reward you with a perfect, velvety set.

👩‍🍳 Steps

  1. Measure out 2 teaspoons powdered gelatin.
  2. Sprinkle evenly over ¼ cup cold water or fruit juice.
  3. Let stand for 5–10 minutes until thick and spongy.
  4. Gently heat — about 10–15 seconds in the microwave or over a warm pan — just until fully dissolved.
  5. Let it cool slightly, then fold into your cheesecake mixture.

★ Pro Tips

  • Never add dry gelatin straight into hot mixtures — it clumps like a toddler with Play-Doh.
  • Don't boil it; gentle heat keeps it behaving.
  • Fruit juice adds flavour that ties your cheesecake together beautifully.
