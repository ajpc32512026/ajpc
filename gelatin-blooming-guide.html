How to Bloom Gelatine

A simple but essential technique for smooth, lump-free cold-set cheesecakes. Treat gelatine right, and it will reward you with a perfect, velvety set.

Print option available at the bottom of the page.

The Process

  1. Measure: Use 2 teaspoons of powdered gelatine for a standard cheesecake.
  2. Sprinkle: Sprinkle the powder evenly over ¼ cup cold water or fruit juice. Do not dump it in a pile.
  3. Bloom: Let it stand for 5–10 minutes until it becomes thick, spongy, and fully hydrated.
  4. Dissolve: Gently heat (10–15 seconds in microwave) just until the granules vanish and the liquid is clear.
  5. Incorporate: Let it cool slightly to room temperature, then fold it into your mixture to avoid "shocking" the cream.

Technique Notes

★ Pro Tips

  • Avoid Clumps: Never add dry gelatine straight into hot mixtures; it will clump instantly.
  • Temperature Control: Do not boil the gelatine. Excessive heat can weaken its setting power.
  • Flavour Boost: Using fruit juice instead of water to bloom the gelatine adds a subtle layer of flavour to your dessert.
